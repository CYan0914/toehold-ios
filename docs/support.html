Toehold Support

Something not working, or something you wish worked differently? Mail support@taomindapp.com. It is a small app run by one person, so you will usually hear back within a couple of days.

Common questions

  • The steps are generic, not about what I typed. Toehold only writes custom steps on devices that can run Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, with the feature on and the model downloaded). Everywhere else it uses built-in self-care step lists, which is why the app never tells you which one ran. Check Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.
  • I get "Couldn't break that down." The on-device model could not answer that request. Repeating it sometimes works, since the model is not deterministic; rewording it also helps. If your device keeps failing, the model may still be downloading.
  • My data disappeared. Tasks live on the device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, the local copy is gone but the iCloud copy usually restores on its own once the app is reinstalled and signed into the same Apple Account.
  • How do I delete everything? Delete the app for the local copy. For the iCloud copy: Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Toehold > Delete Data.
  • Is there a subscription? No. The AI breakdowns run on your own device, so there is no server cost to pass on and nothing to meter. The app is free.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include your device model, your iOS version, and the exact text you typed before it went wrong.

Privacy

Toehold collects no data. The full policy is at the privacy policy.
